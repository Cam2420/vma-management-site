Before you book

A few practical questions.

I already use WhatsApp Business. Why would I need the API?

The WhatsApp Business app gives you a business profile, quick replies, automated greetings and labels to organize chats. The WhatsApp Business Platform (API) connects WhatsApp to other business software, allowing a more customized setup.

VMA builds and manages that setup around your services: an assistant that gathers job details, calendar-connected booking and a shared app where your team can review conversations and take over. Customers still message you through WhatsApp; VMA helps you handle what happens next.

Do I need an existing WhatsApp number?

VMA can provide a dedicated number and guide the setup. We review your current accounts and confirm the number arrangement before launch.

Can I see conversations and take over?

Yes. Your dedicated app access lets you follow inquiries, review replies and pause automation so you or your team can respond personally.

What does the pilot cost?

$0 setup, $450 total advertising budget paid directly to Meta, and a VMA fee of 20% of qualifying collected revenue under the agreed pilot terms. No qualifying revenue means no VMA performance fee; Meta costs still apply. See the full pilot scope, costs and terms.

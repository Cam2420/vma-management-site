Questions

Straight answers.

If being easier to find locally matters, if recent reviews and a strong reputation matter, and if responding quickly to a new customer matters, that's what VMA is built around. If those are not priorities for your business, we're probably not the right fit.

What does it cost?

The VMA Local Growth Engine is $449 per month. There is no setup fee, no long-term contract, and you can cancel any time.

Will this guarantee me more leads?

No honest provider can guarantee a specific number of leads, phone calls, or Google rankings. VMA focuses on improving the factors we can control: how your business shows up online, how strong your reputation looks, and how quickly new opportunities are handled.

How quickly can we get started?

We start with the highest-impact items first. Timing depends on the systems and account access involved, but we'll map the priorities during onboarding and begin implementation as soon as the required access is available.

I already have a website. Do I need a new one?

Usually not. VMA is not a website-only service. We can review your current site and recommend improvements that support local visibility and conversion without rebuilding something that already works.

What do you do for my Google Business Profile?

We review how the profile is set up, identify optimization opportunities, improve the information and content that supports local visibility, and monitor what should be improved over time.

What do you do with reviews?

We help create a consistent review-request process, manage review responses, and strengthen the online reputation customers see when they compare your business with competitors.

What does the automated receptionist do?

It can help respond to new inquiries, collect basic intake information, recover missed calls, answer common questions, and move qualified customers toward scheduling or the appropriate next step.

Is there a contract?

No. The service is month-to-month and can be cancelled at any time.

What do I have to do?

Complete a short onboarding form and provide access to the accounts we need to work on, such as your Google Business Profile, website, social pages, or lead-response systems where applicable. We handle the implementation and ongoing management.

Why use this if word of mouth already works?

Word of mouth is valuable. VMA does not replace it. The goal is to make sure customers who do not already know your business can find you, see enough trust to choose you, and get a fast response when they reach out.

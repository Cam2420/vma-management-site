Before you book

A few practical questions.

What does managed estimate booking include?

It combines done-for-you lead generation with managed follow-up and scheduling. We create and manage your ads, gather project details, check the requirements agreed with you and book suitable estimate appointments. We also manage confirmations, reminders and rescheduling within the agreed service.

Your team approves the campaign, keeps availability current, handles technical questions and attends estimates. You provide the quote, close the sale and complete the work. Qualification is based on the prospect’s answers; your team confirms what work is actually needed.

What does performance-based mean?

For lead generation, VMA’s fee is tied to a qualified estimate appointment actually taking place. An inquiry or a calendar booking alone does not earn that fee. We agree the price per appointment and qualification rules before launch.

Your Meta advertising budget and any separately agreed usage costs still apply. An attended estimate can be billable even if the prospect does not buy. The separate Google Business Profile and review service has a fixed monthly management fee.

See when an appointment counts.
I already use WhatsApp Business. Why would I need the API?

The WhatsApp Business app gives you a business profile, quick replies, automated greetings and labels to organize chats. The WhatsApp Business Platform (API) connects WhatsApp to other business software, allowing a more customized setup.

VMA builds and manages that setup around your services: an assistant that gathers job details, calendar-connected booking and access to the VMA Growth Partner App where your team can review conversations and take over. Customers still message you through WhatsApp; VMA helps you handle what happens next.

Do I need an existing WhatsApp number?

VMA can provide a dedicated number and guide the setup. We review your current accounts and confirm the number arrangement before launch.

Can I see conversations and take over?

Yes. The VMA Growth Partner App lets you follow inquiries, review replies and pause automation so you or your team can respond personally.

What will my team still need to do?

Your team approves the campaign, keeps calendar availability accurate, handles questions requiring trade expertise, attends estimates and records whether appointments took place. You set prices, close the sale and complete the work. VMA manages the agreed advertising, qualification, scheduling and reminders.

What do the two offers cost?

The Lead Generation + Estimate Booking pilot has $0 VMA setup, a $500 total budget paid directly to Meta, and an agreed fee per qualified estimate appointment that takes place. The rate and qualification rules are confirmed before launch. Advertising and any separately agreed usage costs still apply even if no appointment takes place. Google Business Profile + Review Management is a separate $499/month service per location, paid upfront and billed monthly until canceled, with $0 setup. Choose either offer or both.

Before you decide

A few practical questions.

Can I choose just one service?

Yes. Choose Advertising + WhatsApp, Local Visibility + Review Management, or both. The $499 monthly service is separate from your Meta advertising budget and the 10% advertising performance fee.

Any advertising beyond the 30-day pilot is agreed before continuing. The monthly visibility service does not extend your ad campaign. Neither offer guarantees particular rankings, inquiries, bookings or revenue.

What happens if an advertising job is completed after day 30?

The job can still qualify. The inquiry must have come from the pilot ads during the 30 live campaign days, and the eligible payment must be collected during the campaign or within 90 calendar days after the pilot ends.

Inquiry on day 20. Job completed on day 45. Payment on day 60.

If that payment is $1,000 in qualifying collected revenue, VMA’s fee is $100. This is an arithmetic example, not a forecast.

A booking, quote or unpaid invoice does not by itself earn a fee. Payments collected after the 90-day deadline do not qualify for this pilot’s fee. The collection window still applies if you stop advertising; it does not include additional campaign management or ad spend.

Before launch, we agree on eligible jobs and attribution using inquiry-source, job and payment records, including how existing clients, repeat work, deposits, taxes and refunds are handled. The 10% fee does not apply to all your business revenue. Read the qualifying revenue terms.

What does month-to-month billing mean?

Local Visibility + Review Management is $499 per month per location, with $0 setup. The first month is paid before work begins. You then continue to be billed monthly until you cancel, without a long-term commitment.

We confirm the service start date and monthly billing schedule at signup. Email growth@vmamgmt.com before your next billing date to stop future monthly charges. Cancellation, service-end and refund details are set out in your agreement before payment.

What will my team need to provide?

Accurate business information, appropriate account access, approvals and photos or other materials you have permission to use.

For advertising, your team responds to handovers, sets prices, performs the work and keeps job and payment records current. For local visibility, you share business changes, completed-job details and the permissions needed for review requests, and help resolve feedback that needs your attention.

We agree on the covered profiles, content schedule, review-response scope and any additional costs before starting.

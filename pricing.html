Before you decide

A few practical questions.

Can I choose just one service?

Yes. Choose Lead Generation + Estimate Booking, Google Business Profile + Review Management, or both. The $499 monthly service is separate from your Meta advertising budget and any attended-estimate fees.

Any advertising beyond the 30-day pilot is agreed before continuing. The monthly visibility service does not extend your ad campaign. Neither offer guarantees particular rankings, inquiries, bookings or revenue.

What counts as a qualified attended estimate?

A qualified prospect has expressed interest in the agreed service, has a property in your service area and meets the project requirements we set with you. We ask whether they are authorized to arrange the estimate, gather their contact and property details, and confirm an available time.

The estimate appointment must meet those agreed requirements and actually take place with your team and the prospect. Qualification is based on the information provided. It does not confirm a technical diagnosis, ability to pay or intention to buy. Your team assesses the property and decides the scope and price.

The written agreement sets the fee per appointment, attendance evidence, duplicate and existing-inquiry rules, billing schedule and how disputed appointments are reviewed. A qualifying attended estimate can be billable even if the prospect does not buy. Read the appointment terms.

What if someone cancels, does not attend or reschedules?

An appointment that does not take place does not earn an attended-estimate fee. A rescheduled appointment is assessed when it actually takes place and is not counted twice for the same estimate.

Your team keeps availability accurate, attends confirmed appointments and updates attendance records. We agree how cancellations, missed visits, disputed attendance and any billing corrections are handled before launch.

What if an appointment happens after the 30-day pilot?

We record which inquiries came from the pilot ads. Before launch, we agree a final date for eligible appointments and a follow-up period for those inquiries. An appointment after the campaign ends counts only if it takes place within that agreed window and meets the qualification rules.

The end of the ad campaign does not make every later appointment billable. Ongoing ad management and any additional spending require a separate agreement.

Does $500 guarantee a certain number of appointments?

No. It is the total starting advertising budget for the pilot, not a promise of appointment volume. Results depend on the project type, area, offer, competition and customer response. We review the results with you before recommending further spend.

What does month-to-month billing mean?

Google Business Profile + Review Management is $499 per month per location, with $0 setup. The first month is paid before work begins. You then continue to be billed monthly until you cancel, without a long-term commitment.

We confirm the service start date and monthly billing schedule at signup. Email growth@vmamgmt.com before your next billing date to stop future monthly charges. Cancellation, service-end and refund details are set out in your agreement before payment.

What will my team need to provide?

Accurate business information, appropriate account access, approvals and photos or other materials you have permission to use.

For estimate booking, your team keeps calendar availability accurate, handles technical questions, attends estimates and confirms attendance. You provide quotes, close sales and perform the work. For local visibility, you share business changes, completed-job details and the permissions needed for review requests, and help resolve feedback that needs your attention.

We agree on the covered profiles, content schedule, review-response scope and any additional costs before starting.

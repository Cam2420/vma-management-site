Before you decide

A few practical questions.

What happens if no qualifying revenue is collected?

You owe no VMA performance fee. The Meta advertising cost still applies. We do not guarantee a specific number of leads, bookings, paid jobs or profit.

Will Meta refund the advertising budget if the pilot does not produce results?

Meta does not give refunds for poor ad performance or return on investment. Other refunds, including eligible requests for unused available funds, depend on Meta's rules and decision. VMA does not collect your $450 advertising budget or guarantee a Meta refund.

See Meta's types of advertising refunds (opens in a new tab) and refund eligibility guidance (opens in a new tab).

What must my business handle?

You provide accurate service information, required access and approvals. Your team responds when a person is needed, confirms scope and prices, performs the work and keeps job and payment records current. You pay Meta directly.

When do the 30 days begin?

The pilot covers 30 live campaign days. We confirm the launch date after setup and required approvals. Platform reviews and account requirements can affect readiness; there is no fixed approval timeline.

What happens after the pilot?

We review the progress summary and discuss the next step. We will explain any proposed ongoing service and agree its scope, costs and terms before you choose whether to continue.

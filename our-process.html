Setup questions

No technical guesswork required.

Is this just the WhatsApp Business App?

No. VMA configures the WhatsApp Business Platform (API) with connected intake, permitted follow-up and team handover. The Business App is a separate product; a basic app installation is not the service offered here.

Do I need an existing WhatsApp number?

No. VMA can provide a dedicated business number and guide its WhatsApp registration. If you already have a number or account, we review whether it is suitable to reuse. We confirm number availability, registration requirements and any applicable costs before launch.

Does the assistant know my business?

We train it on your approved services, business hours, service area and answers to common questions. We also configure what it should collect, when it can offer a booking and when your team should take over. You review the answers before launch and tell us when details change.

Can my team review photos customers send?

Customers can share supported photos in a WhatsApp inquiry. Receiving a file does not mean an automated system can diagnose the job or provide a reliable quote from it.

How do you check which appointments qualify?

Before launch, we agree the service, area and project requirements, then record the prospect’s answers, confirmation and attendance. VMA’s appointment fee applies when an estimate meets those requirements and takes place. Your team still determines what work is needed; an appointment is not a guaranteed sale.
